Support
PAMA offers a wide range of after-sales services developed to guarantee maximum machine tool reliability and productivity.
Customers are provided with a wide range of services and contracts to guarantee the highest performance of their machines over time: from remote help line and diagnostics to maintenance contracts, from preventive check-ups to maintenance with original spare parts and dedicated spare parts kits, concluding with complete retrofitting of PAMA machines.
The capillarity of these services should be emphasised: PAMA’s service network is developed thanks to branches operating in all major world markets.
