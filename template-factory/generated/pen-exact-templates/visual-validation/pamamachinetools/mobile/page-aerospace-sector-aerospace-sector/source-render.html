AEROSPACE SECTOR
PAMA offers products tested and developed for the processing of hard materials such as titanium, Inconel and steel alloys, the main applications found in the aerospace sector .
PAMA’s machine tools , in a horizontal or vertical configuration, in combination with 5-axis and multitasking machining, as well as a wide selection of CNC attachment heads, guarantee complex machining with extremely high levels of precision and finishing in a sector as complex as the aerospace industry .
PAMA Speedmat VM machining centers – with a gantry structure – are able to combine high dynamics and rigidity and the highest workpiece precision.
PAMA Speedmat HP machining centers – with a T-type structure are able to combine maximum dynamics and rigidity and high working precision
