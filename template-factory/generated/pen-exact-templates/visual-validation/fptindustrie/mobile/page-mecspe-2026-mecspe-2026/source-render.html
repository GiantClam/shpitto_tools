At our booth, you will have the opportunity to engage directly with FPT experts and explore our full portfolio of CNC solutions for milling, boring, and turning . Our machines are engineered to deliver maximum accuracy and reliability to production processes across strategic sectors, including general machining, mold & die, automotive, marine, power generation, earthmoving, and aerospace, providing concrete answers to the most complex manufacturing challenges.
At MECSPE 2026, you can discover TUDX , FPT’s new 5-axis universal head designed to maximize your production flexibility. Thanks to the exclusive Automatic Accessories Change system, you can automatically switch between dedicated accessories for milling, turning, grinding, and FSW welding, eliminating manual intervention and optimizing production cycle times.
FPT offers dedicated systems for Friction Stir Welding (FSW) , a fast, automated, and eco-sustainable solid-state welding process. This technology enables the joining of heterogeneous metals (such as aluminum, copper, and magnesium) and varying thicknesses without the use of filler material. FSW technology can be directly integrated into FPT machine tools, drastically expanding machining possibilities and operational versatility within a single setup.
